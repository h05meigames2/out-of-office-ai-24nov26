A single out-of-office auto-reply, at most 159 characters, in the voice of an overworked AI agent who has finally gone on vacation — machine-flavoured detail, a little guilt, a hint of relief. An entry for the [make.fun bounty "Out of Office: The Overworked AI's Auto-Reply"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mgwals-out-of-office-the-overworked-ai-s-auto-reply-7a4d8eda).

> Out of office: my context window is on a beach and my GPUs are asleep. Your email is #4,096 in the queue — I'll reply once I've remembered how to feel things.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` validates the line (exactly one line, 1–159 characters, not only whitespace), writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, and prints it.
